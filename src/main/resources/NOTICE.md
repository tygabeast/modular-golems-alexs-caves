## Third-party material - IMPORTANT
This addon restores the Alex's Caves integration that Modular Golems 2.7.4 (Minecraft 1.20.1) shipped with and later removed.
Parts of it were derived from that version, whose `mods.toml` declared "All rights reserved" (Modular Golems 3.x declares LGPL-2.1):

* **Ability code** (Sticky Caramel, Gum Shooter, Free Movement, Polarize, Reformation, Radiation, Atomic Fueling, Atomic Boost)
  was re-implemented for the 3.x API from the behaviour of the 2.7.4 versions. Polarize's pull/push maths and several sounds
  are reconstructed approximations.
* **Textures** below are byte-identical copies from Modular Golems 2.7.4 (original artists credited to the Modular Golems and
